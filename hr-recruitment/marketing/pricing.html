Frequently Asked Questions

Is there really no credit card required?+
Absolutely. Your 14-day free trial requires no credit card. You only enter payment details when you choose to upgrade after the trial.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes. Cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings with no penalties or cancellation fees.
How accurate are the psychometric tests?+
Our test framework is developed based on professional psychology standards. The questions cover logical reasoning, personality assessment, and situational judgment — giving a well-rounded view of candidates.
Does it work in Bahasa Indonesia?+
The platform is designed for the Indonesian market. The candidate experience and test questions are fully available in Bahasa Indonesia on the Pro and Enterprise plans.
Can candidates take the test on mobile?+
Yes. The test interface is fully responsive and works on any device — smartphone, tablet, or desktop. The anti-cheat system works on all platforms.
