1. Who we are & how to contact us

BRICKX Protocol operates the BRICKX websites and token platform and is the controller responsible for the personal data described in this Policy. For any privacy question, request, or complaint, contact us at privacy@brickxprotocol.io.

2. Personal data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

a) Account & profile data

  • Full name, email address, and self-declared country of residence.
  • Password — stored only as a salted one-way hash (bcrypt); we never store or see your plaintext password.
  • Account metadata: account status, email-verification (OTP) status, referral code, and the referral code of whoever invited you (if any).

b) Identity & verification (KYC) data

  • Government-issued identity documents (e.g., passport, national ID), a selfie/liveness image, and the data extracted from them (such as name, date of birth, document number, nationality), together with the verification result and status.
  • This data is collected and processed by our identity-verification provider (Sumsub). Where manual verification is used before our provider is live, documents you upload are stored in a private, access-controlled location and reviewed by authorized staff.

c) Transaction & financial data

  • Your orders (amount, selected currency, round, price, allocation), order status, and the public blockchain transaction hashes associated with your payments.
  • The blockchain wallet address you register to receive tokens and from which you pay.

d) Blockchain & on-chain data

  • Public addresses, transaction amounts, and timestamps that exist on the Polygon blockchain. This data is public by nature (see Section 8).

e) Technical & usage data

  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device type, operating system, browser type, and language.
  • Server and access logs, pages viewed, actions taken, referring URLs, and timestamps.
  • Cookie and analytics identifiers (see Section 5).

f) Communications & support data

  • The content of emails and support messages you send us, and our responses.
  • Records of service emails we send you (e.g., verification codes, order and payment confirmations, distribution notices).

We do not intentionally collect special-category data (such as health, religion, or political views). Please do not send us such data.

3. How we collect it

  • Directly from you — when you register, verify your email, complete KYC, register a wallet, place an order, or contact us.
  • Automatically — through cookies, analytics, and server logs as you use the Services.
  • From third parties — verification results from our KYC provider, and on-chain payment data read from the public blockchain.

5. Cookies, analytics & tracking

Our websites use cookies and similar technologies. Strictly necessary cookies keep you signed in and the site functioning. We also use Google Analytics, which sets cookies/identifiers to measure traffic and usage in aggregate; this helps us understand and improve the Services.

You can control cookies through your browser settings and can opt out of Google Analytics using Google's opt-out browser add-on. Disabling some cookies may affect site functionality. Where required by law, we will request your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

7. International data transfers

BRICKX operates internationally and our providers may store or process your data in countries other than your own. Those countries may have data-protection laws that differ from yours. Where we transfer personal data across borders, we take steps to ensure an appropriate level of protection, such as using providers that offer recognized safeguards (for example, standard contractual clauses) and applying technical and organizational security measures.

8. Blockchain data — important notice

Payments and token allocations are recorded on the public Polygon blockchain. Information written to a blockchain — including wallet addresses, transaction amounts, and timestamps — is public, permanent, and outside our control. It cannot be changed, hidden, or deleted by us or by you, and may be visible to anyone and linked to other information. Please consider this before transacting. Our deletion and erasure obligations do not, and cannot, extend to data recorded on a public blockchain.

9. How long we keep your data

  • Account data — for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Services.
  • KYC/AML records — retained for the period required by applicable anti-money-laundering laws (commonly several years, e.g. up to 5 years) after your relationship with us ends, even if you close your account.
  • Transaction records — retained as needed for accounting, audit, and legal/tax obligations.
  • Technical/analytics data — retained for a limited period and/or held in aggregate form.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or irreversibly anonymize it. Data on the blockchain remains as described in Section 8.

10. How we protect your data

  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes (bcrypt); session tokens are signed and time-limited.
  • Data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access to databases is restricted by row-level security and role-based controls; sensitive keys are held server-side only and never exposed to the browser.
  • KYC documents are kept in a private, access-controlled store and viewed only via short-lived, authenticated links by authorized staff, or held by our KYC provider under its own controls.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we apply reasonable safeguards, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a personal-data breach that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where required by law.

11. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access — obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data (subject to our legal retention obligations and the blockchain limitation in Section 8).
  • Restriction — ask us to limit processing in certain circumstances.
  • Portability — receive certain data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time (without affecting prior processing).
  • Complain — lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority (see Section 15).

To exercise any right, email privacy@brickxprotocol.io. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. Exercising your rights is free, although we may charge a reasonable fee or decline manifestly unfounded or excessive requests where the law allows.

12. Children's privacy

The Services are intended only for persons aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

These may affect whether your verification is approved. Where automated processing produces a legal or similarly significant effect on you, you may request human review by contacting us, subject to applicable law.
