Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how BRICKX Protocol ("we") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our websites and platform.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, country, password (stored only as a salted hash).
  • KYC data: identity documents and verification results, processed by our KYC provider (Sumsub).
  • Transaction data: orders, amounts, and your provided blockchain wallet address.
  • Technical data: IP address, device/browser info, and usage logs.

2. How We Use It

  • To operate the token sale, deliver allocations, and provide support.
  • To meet KYC/AML and other legal obligations.
  • To detect fraud and secure the platform.
  • To send service emails (confirmations, status updates).

3. Sharing

We share data only with processors needed to run the service — KYC (Sumsub), email (Resend), hosting/database (Railway, Supabase, Vercel) — and with authorities where legally required. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Storage & Security

Passwords are hashed (bcrypt). Sensitive keys are held server-side only. KYC documents are handled by our provider under their security controls. No system is perfectly secure; we apply reasonable safeguards.

5. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or export of your data, and may object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights. Note we may retain certain records to meet legal/AML obligations.

6. Retention

We keep data for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal requirements, then delete or anonymize it.

7. Contact

Data requests: privacy@brickxprotocol.io
