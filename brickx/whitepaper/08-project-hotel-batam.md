## 8.1 Why Buy an Existing Hotel

| Immediate Revenue | Hotel is already operational. Cash flow begins from day one of acquisition. |
|---|---|
| Proven Performance | Historical occupancy rates, ADR, and full P&L financials available for due diligence. |
| Lower Risk | No construction risk, no permitting delays, no 2-3 year wait before first revenue. |
| Capital Efficiency | USD 12.3M-18.5M for existing hotel vs USD 18.5M-24.7M to build equivalent new. |
| Upgrade Strategy | Acquire 3-star hotel, renovate and rebrand to 4-star standard — value creation opportunity. |

## 8.2 Why Batam, Indonesia

* Strategic location: 30-minute high-speed ferry from Singapore — one of Asia's wealthiest cities
* Indonesia Free Trade Zone: simplified permits, tax advantages, and ease of doing business
* Growing demand: Hotel demand in Batam growing approximately 15% annually driven by business travel and Singapore overflow tourism
* Supply gap: Limited supply of quality 4-star hotels relative to growing demand — creates pricing power
* Affordable acquisition: Properties 40-60% cheaper than equivalent assets in Jakarta, Bali, or Singapore
* Local expertise: BRICKX founder has 10+ years of active real estate development experience in Batam, including current hotel renovation project management — irreplaceable local network
