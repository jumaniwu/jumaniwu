## 13.2 KYC and AML Compliance

All users must complete Know Your Customer (KYC) verification before purchasing any tokens. BRICKX partners with Sumsub for identity verification. Supported documents: passport, national ID, and driver's license from 80+ countries. Users from sanctioned jurisdictions are blocked at the KYC stage.

## 13.3 Annual Audit Requirement

Hotel financial statements are audited annually by an independent licensed auditor before any dividend distribution. Audit reports are published publicly on the BRICKX website. This ensures full transparency and protects investor interests.
