# 6. Tokenomics

## 6.1 BRX Token Distribution

Total Supply: 1,000,000,000 BRX — Fixed forever. No additional minting possible after genesis.

| Ecosystem & Staking | 350,000,000 (35%) — Platform staking rewards and ecosystem incentives |
|---|---|
| ICO Public Sale | 150,000,000 (15%) — Rounds 1, 2, and DEX liquidity |
| Team (3-year vested) | 120,000,000 (12%) — 12-month cliff, then 24-month linear vest |
| Strategic Partners | 100,000,000 (10%) — Advisors and development partners, vested |
| Seed Sale | 80,000,000 (8%) — Current round, 12-month lock + 24-month linear vest |
| Marketing & KOL | 80,000,000 (8%) — Community growth and influencer partnerships |
| DEX Liquidity (Locked) | 70,000,000 (7%) — Locked in smart contract for QuickSwap liquidity |
| DAO Reserve | 50,000,000 (5%) — Governed by token holders via DAO vote |
| Total | 1,000,000,000 (100%) |

## 6.3 Deflationary Mechanisms

BRX implements multiple burn mechanisms to create long-term scarcity and reward long-term holders:

* 30% of all platform listing fees collected in BRX are permanently burned
* 5% of BRICK token secondary market transaction fees converted to BRX and burned
* Failed governance proposals result in 10% of the proposal bond being burned
* Annual buyback-and-burn program funded from protocol's 30% NOI share
