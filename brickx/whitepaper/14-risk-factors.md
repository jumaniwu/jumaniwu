# 14. Risk Factors

Investing in BRICKX Protocol involves significant risks. Participants should carefully consider the following before investing:

* Hotel Operating Risk: Hotel revenue and NOI may be lower than projected due to market conditions, competition, management performance, or macroeconomic factors.
* Regulatory Risk: Changes in cryptocurrency regulation, property ownership laws, or foreign investment rules in Indonesia or Singapore could affect operations.
* Smart Contract Risk: Despite audits, smart contracts may contain undiscovered vulnerabilities. BRICKX is not responsible for losses resulting from smart contract exploits.
* Exchange Rate Risk: Hotel revenue is earned in Indonesian Rupiah. Exchange rate fluctuations between IDR and USD affect dividend amounts.
* Liquidity Risk: While BRICKX provides a marketplace, there is no guarantee that buyers will always be available for BRICK token listings.
* Key Person Risk: BRICKX's Phase 2 hotel acquisition strategy depends heavily on the founder's local expertise and relationships in Batam. Loss of key personnel could affect execution.
* ICO Completion Risk: If Phase 1 ICO does not reach its target, hotel acquisition will not proceed and investors will receive full refunds.
