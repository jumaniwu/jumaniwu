# 2. The Solution

BRICKX Protocol is a blockchain-native platform that tokenizes real hotel properties and makes fractional ownership accessible to anyone with an internet connection, a cryptocurrency wallet, and as little as USD 100.

Investors purchase BRICK tokens representing fractional ownership of a specific hotel property. Each BRICK token is priced at exactly USD 10.00 permanently — this price never changes. Returns come entirely from real hotel operating income, distributed annually in USDC every June.

## 2.1 Core Value Propositions

| Fixed Token Price | USD 10.00 per BRICK token — permanent. Zero price speculation. Returns from yield only. |
|---|---|
| Annual Dividend | 70% of Net Operating Income distributed to holders every June in USDC. |
| Audited Cycle | December 31 fiscal close → independent audit Q1 → April announcement → June payment. |
| Marketplace Exit | List BRICK tokens on marketplace at USD 10.00 anytime. No lock period after purchase. |
| Minimum USD 100 | Accessible to any investor globally. No local bank account or residency required. |
| Polygon Network | Gas fees under USD 0.01. 2-minute confirmation. USDT/USDC/ETH/BNB accepted. |

## 2.2 What Makes BRICKX Different

Most real estate tokenization platforms are either speculative (token price fluctuates with market) or impractical (complex processes, high minimums, limited markets). BRICKX takes a fundamentally different approach:

* Zero speculation: BRICK tokens have a permanent fixed price. Your investment thesis is simple — hold BRICK, earn hotel income annually.
* Real assets: Phase 2 acquires an existing operating hotel with a proven revenue track record — not a hypothetical future project.
* Southeast Asia focus: The highest-growth, most underserved real estate markets with the largest untapped investor base.
* Transparent governance: All hotel financials are independently audited annually before dividend distributions. Full audit reports published publicly.
