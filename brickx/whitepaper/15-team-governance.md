## 15.2 DAO Governance

Upon completing Phase 1 (Q3 2026), BRICKX will transition critical protocol decisions to a fully on-chain DAO. BRX token holders vote on:

* New hotel property acquisition approvals and due diligence standards
* Fee structure modifications
* Treasury fund allocation and BRX buyback-and-burn schedules
* Protocol upgrades and smart contract changes
* New market expansion priorities and geographic focus

Governance parameters: 1 BRX = 1 vote. Minimum 1,000 BRX required to submit proposals. Quorum of 10% of circulating supply required for binding votes.
