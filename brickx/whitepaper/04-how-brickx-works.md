# 4. How BRICKX Works

## 4.1 Investor Journey — Step by Step

* Register: Create account at brickxprotocol.io. Provide email and set password.
* Add Wallet: Register your Polygon wallet address. This wallet receives BRX at TGE and BRICK tokens upon purchase, and receives USDC annual dividends.
* Complete KYC: Verify identity via Sumsub (5 minutes). Supports passport, national ID, and driver's license from 80+ countries. Required before any purchase.
* Purchase BRX (Phase 1): Send USDT, USDC, ETH, or BNB to the BRICKX treasury wallet. System auto-detects payment on-chain via Polygon. BRX allocated to your wallet at TGE.
* Access Platform (After TGE): Use BRX to access the BRICKX marketplace. Purchase BRICK hotel tokens at USD 10.00 fixed price.
* Earn Annual Dividend: Hotel generates revenue throughout the year. At December 31, a snapshot of all BRICK token holders is taken. After the Q1 independent audit, dividend is announced in April and USDC is automatically distributed to all holder wallets in June.
* Exit When Ready: List BRICK tokens on the BRICKX marketplace at USD 10.00 anytime. No lock period after initial purchase. Buyer purchases instantly and USDC is credited to your wallet.

## 4.2 Property Tokenization Process

Every hotel property on BRICKX goes through a multi-step process before BRICK tokens are issued:

* Due Diligence: Minimum 90-day review of legal title, environmental assessments, building condition, financial records, and local regulatory compliance.
* SPV Structure: A dedicated Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) legal entity is established in the jurisdiction of the property to hold title and operate the hotel.
* Independent Valuation: Third-party property valuation by a licensed appraisal firm establishes acquisition price and projected NOI.
* Token Issuance: BRICK tokens are minted via the BRICK ERC-1155 smart contract on Polygon at USD 10.00 each. Total tokens = acquisition cost divided by USD 10.00.
* Marketplace Listing: BRICK tokens are listed on the BRICKX marketplace. Investors purchase at USD 10.00 fixed price.
