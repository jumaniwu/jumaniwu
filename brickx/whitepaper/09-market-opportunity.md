## 9.2 Why Now

Several macro trends are converging to make the BRICKX launch timing optimal:

* Institutional RWA momentum: BlackRock, JPMorgan, and Franklin Templeton have all launched tokenized real-world asset products. The infrastructure for institutional credibility is established.
* DeFi yield compression: On-chain DeFi yields have compressed significantly. Real-world yield from hotel income (5-8% APY) is increasingly attractive to sophisticated crypto investors.
* Polygon maturity: Polygon has processed billions of transactions with proven security, scale, and reliability. Gas fees remain under USD 0.01.
* Regulatory clarity: MiCA in Europe, improving SEC guidance in the United States, and crypto-friendly policies in Singapore and Indonesia create a more navigable compliance environment.
* Mobile penetration: Internet and smartphone penetration in Southeast Asia now exceeds 70%, creating a massive new addressable investor base.
