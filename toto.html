Read this first: TOTO draws are independent random events. Past results carry no information about future draws, so no analysis here — or anywhere — can improve your odds of winning (1 in 13,983,816 for the jackpot). This tool describes what has happened; it does not predict what will happen. The built-in backtest measures the generator against pure chance so you can see that for yourself. Play only what you can afford to lose.

Does it actually work?

Walk-forward test: for each draw, the model is rebuilt from only the draws before it, sets are generated, and they are scored against the real result. A pure-random control runs on the same draws.
